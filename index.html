Milestones

The road to Murph: 1-mi run · 100 pull-ups · 200 push-ups · 300 squats · 1-mi run, then the 20-lb vest. Each tier is a bigger fraction of those targets. They tick themselves as your workouts and benchmark tests reach the numbers — and you can tap any of them to set it by hand.

Consistency

One bar per week — how many sessions you logged. Each label is the Sunday that week began (so “7/12” = the week of Sun Jul 12). Showing the last 8 weeks; consistency beats intensity, so the aim is a steady row, not one tall bar.

Effort trend

The difficulty you rated each session (1–10) when you logged it, in order — nothing is calculated or inferred. The dashed line is your average across every session. Drifting down over time at the same workload means it's getting easier: that's fitness.
